# Distinct provisional novel title-lead index

The canonical production series catalogue is 66 verified source-linked originals.
The separate 7,041-item research archive has 2,810 author placeholders and 97
possible non-novels. The published provisional explorer strips placeholder
authors, obvious noisy titles, and the already verified Chinese records,
retaining around 4,094 entries; the exact generated number is CI evidence.

This is NOT 4,094 more verified webnovels, a chapter corpus, or a publisher feed.
The dataset claims CC0 but cannot grant rights to publisher descriptions,
cover images or translated chapters. The index contains title, reported author
and broad category hint only. No invented official source URLs are inserted.
Source: https://github.com/HeTaobaba123/arabic-chinese-novels-dataset/releases/tag/v1.0.0
