## Why some statuses are unknown

Royal Road `STUB` refers to availability/removal of chapters, **not necessarily completion of the underlying novel**.
`INACTIVE` likewise does not prove permanent discontinuation. The Last Orellen's author describes a hiatus.
Only Ar'Kendrithyst is explicitly labelled `COMPLETED` among these ten records.
The original URL should be used to check where readers can currently obtain the work.
External novels remain on their original platforms; Novalume hosts no text, artwork or third-party reviews.
